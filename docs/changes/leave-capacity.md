**Changed — capacity and planned figures change for months with planned leave**
- **Leave and holidays come off the capacity when planned** — every leave or holiday Task (your own PTO, sick, unplanned and other leave, your own holiday rows and the shared regional or everyone holidays) now counts by its **actual if recorded, else planned**: Completed Work once booked, the Original Estimate until then (before: your own leave only once booked; shared holidays plan first). So a month with leave planned but not yet booked shows a **lower Capacity** on Time Writing, My dashboard, Team overview, Pulse Check, Capacity and actual and the Monthly pack, and a shared holiday with fewer hours booked than planned counts the booked hours. Common activities keep their plan first. Months whose figures are locked keep their locked figures. Calculation profiles: the deducted Absence bucket and a typed formula's [Absence] follow the rule; the Old Calculation is unchanged (CAP-63, replaces CAP-03; CAP-11, CAP-38).
- **Planned and Actual hours without leave and holidays** — on Time Writing the Planned hours and Actual hours tiles (and their % of capacity) no longer include leave and holidays, which are already taken off the capacity; trainings, other NPRA and common activities stay in (CAP-64, ACT-11). Example, November 2026: 168 h contractual, 88 h leave planned, a 16 h regional holiday, 40 h work planned — Capacity 64 h (was 152 h), Planned 40 h (was 128 h), 63 % (was 84 %).
- **The Capacity tile names what was taken off** — e.g. "168h contractual − 88h leave − 16h holiday · leave and holidays: actual if recorded, else planned"; My dashboard's My hours ⓘ gives the same line (ACT-28).
